
## Our Wonderful Story

1. Once upon a time, there was a **pirate**.  
   ![pirate](img/pirate.png)

2. Who wants to be king of all the pirates across the seven seas.so he set sail guided by an ancient, mysterious **map**.  
   ![map](img/map.png)
   
3. The **scientist** shouted, "Stop! That pirate map points straight to the cursed treasure of the seven seas!" but nobody listened because the pirate was already fighting a giant dragon .
   ![scientist](img/scientist.png)

4. During the chaotic fight, the dragon accidentally smashed a secret mechanism on the ground, triggering a magical explosion that teleported everyone onto a mysterious island—right in front of a locked **treasure_chest**!
   ![treasure chest](img/treasure_large.png)
   

5. After unlocking the treasure chest ,It  was full of treasure.
 ![treasure chest open](img/treasure_open.png) 
 But the pirate cared about only one thing inside is another map, labeled "Space." He found out it was a continuation of the first map he had found.
     ![map](img/map.png)

6. Even though he had no idea what space was,It still thrilled him because he loved a challenge. “Let’s go to space!” he shouted.

7. The pirate and his friends boarded the spaceship standing next to the treasure chest and headed toward the destination shown on the mysterious map.
     ![rocket](img/rocket.png)

8. Upon arriving at an unknown, mysterious planet, they found themselves surrounded by scorching heat, reddish-orange brick-colored ground, and strange structures. There, they encountered a green creature that looked nothing like a human and was holding an ice cream cone. It introduced itself as a citizen of Planet CS100 and wanted to negotiate with them. 
     ![alien](img/alien.png)

9. The alien suggested, "Our planet CS100 is running out of ice cream, and only a true king can solve this problem. If you help us, we will make you the king of the galaxy."
The pirate and his friends looked at each other, then the pirate said, "Alright, but I get to eat ice cream for free for life." The alien grinned and lifted a crown made of waffle cones.

10. Just as the crown touched his head, there was a loud "POOF!" The pirate woke up on his ship back on Earth, with his friends snoring beside him. A grinning **wizard** stood there with a sparkling wand. "Sorry," he said, "I cast a sleeping spell on you all because you kept arguing about who should be king." The pirate looked around. No alien, no CS100, no ice cream. But there was one waffle cone stuck in his hair.
      ![wizard](img/wizard.png)
